## 3. Roles, registration, and permissions

**Only members self-register.** `AuthService` fixes `role='member'`; public input cannot request an officer/admin account. `pending_registrations` lasts 24 hours; the code lasts 10 minutes, bounded by staging expiry on resend. OTP success creates an active, email-verified, membership-pending member. Existing legacy accounts may still log in with unverified email; login does not itself require membership or email verification.

**Admin Officer provisioning is controlled maintenance, not public registration or membership verification.** Migration 024 historically converted officers to global admins. Migration 027 and current middleware then narrow staff access to the designated existing account (`markseejr@gmail.com`). Historical [seed_demo_users.php](../database/seed_demo_users.php) explicitly promotes that address; later [application rollout](CHAT_APPLICATION_ROLLOUT_RESULTS.md) records retaining one designated admin. Fresh SELECTs confirm one active admin and the single-admin triggers, but do not reconstruct its original creation. There is no current staff creation/transfer UI, public staff signup, or working promotion endpoint. A reviewed responsibility transfer remains an unresolved process. Never describe member ID approval as officer-account provisioning.

## 4. Current end-to-end and alternate flows

1. Member stages registration with privacy acknowledgment, identity/chapter/DOB and optional blood/location/ID; verifies OTP; logs in; edits allowed profile fields, picture and canonical location; submits National ID and optional donor card/parental consent. Admin Officer reviews, approves or rejects with reason. Rejected members upload/resubmit for another decision; a legacy unverified member moves to pending on National ID upload. Upload requires the document privacy acknowledgment, and National ID replacement is locked after membership verification. Donor-card acceptance records administrative provenance, not medical verification.
2. An email-verified active pending/verified requester creates a routine/urgent/emergency request with quantity, facility/location and future deadline. Request persists even if automatic matching fails; response reports `failed_retry_required`. Admin rematch is available. Owner views requests and restricted donor results in counted Donor offers (default) and Suggested donors tabs, with search/chapter/status filtering and five entries per tab page; eligible donors view compatible requests and their own match. Suggested donors can receive a message request but cannot be accepted as an offer until they actually respond. Material edits reconcile matches and preserve claims needing explicit review.
3. Donors enroll after platform age/consent checks (under 16 blocked; ages 16–17 require consent on file; adult prerequisites still apply). They control available/unavailable state. New offers recheck current eligibility under locks. Home and Matches ask for confirmation; responding directly to a compatible request can create a match without a prior alert. Withdrawal requires an active response and no pending claim; a later response starts a new offer cycle.
4. The verified requester may accept a current eligible offer to create/reuse pair chat consent and a request/offer-cycle context. Acceptance is not donation credit. Existing chat consent survives donor cooldown, standby or report holds while both accounts remain active/verified. Participants can indicate still arranging/did not happen; requester may prompt the donor at most once in 24 hours. Ordinary messaging uses separate request/accept/decline/cancel consent with seven-day pending expiry and one-day retry after decline/cancel. Chat supports suggestions, paged history, unread/read state and session-scoped presence. Admin cannot browse arbitrary private conversations; reported messages expose only a bounded case snapshot, with self-involvement review blocked.
5. Matched verified donor submits actual donation date/time (Asia/Manila picker to UTC), optional note and up to three protected proof photos. Occurrence cannot precede response or be future. Reports can concern RESPONDED or CLOSED matches; historical recording is separate from readiness for a new offer. Submission creates a report-specific hold, not credit.
6. Requester acknowledges the exact version: received may complete mutually; did not donate/not sure requires review. Missing dates/invalidation/clarification require exceptional review. Admin independently confirms with basis, rejects with reason or requests clarification; participants reply, and donor revises in clarification. Revised versions require fresh acknowledgment. Proof updates retain earlier evidence/version history and do not erase disputes. Pending reports survive terminal requests. Rejection alone retains its hold; explicit no-donation finding releases only that rejected report's hold. Completed-record correction records staff response without reversing credit/fulfillment/restrictions.
7. Completion records one tracking unit per completed match, occurrence-based restriction anchor and history atomically. Only a still-open, unexpired request meeting quantity becomes FULFILLED. Late genuine completions can update history/restrictions without reopening cancelled/expired/fulfilled requests. Owner may cancel or close with need-met-elsewhere/no-longer-needed reason, awarding no credit. Expiry closes unfinished offers; renewal creates one linked replacement for an expired request, preserving old history (an active request renewal action instead updates its deadline). Owner can request staff donation follow-up. Unacknowledged claims have 24-hour reminder/48-hour escalation processing; silence never confirms/rejects.
8. Users receive in-app event notifications, popup/flyout/list, deep links, unread counts and mark-one/all-read controls. Approved email classes enter a durable outbox; workers revalidate event eligibility, retry/defer/cancel, group emergency mail and reserve attempt budgets. Staff monitor global/chapter demand, operational metrics, report queues, analytics/CSV/print and protected audit filters. Member closure requires password plus DELETE; retention erases eligible private data after defined delays while preserving unresolved claims/investigations.

Primary implementation entry points are [API routes](../backend/routes/api.php), [React routes](../frontend/src/App.jsx), [completion service](../backend/src/Services/DonationCompletionService.php), and [research coverage matrix](research/SOP_OBJECTIVES_FEATURE_AUDIT.md#2-feature-coverage-matrix).

## 7. Remaining gaps and conflicts

1. **Research source missing:** no current SOP/objectives manuscript was found among repository documents; no DOCX/PDF manuscript was found either. The new research document is a complete proposed replacement, not a verified line edit. Original development/testing/evaluation wording and the required evaluation standard need confirmation.
2. **Baseline vs approved role model:** baseline requires three roles and general administration. Current approved consolidation plus later single-admin maintenance supports two stored roles and one designated global Admin Officer. No ordinary promotion or account-transfer workflow exists; sole-admin self-involved cases have no second independent reviewer.
3. **Matching semantics:** compatibility substitutions work, with ordered normalized pairs, but MatchService ranks the whole compatible pool by location/chapter/proximity; it does not execute sequential biological tiers. Chapter/municipality are ranking, not hard candidate exclusions. Approved no-hard-exclusion policy and baseline filtering/cascading language must be reconciled. Home request-card filters do not prove candidate filtering.
4. **Readiness vs stored availability:** new offers correctly use the live evaluator. `ProfileController::setDonorAvailability` checks active/enrolled/windows but not role/membership/email, and repeats setAvailability outside its locked transaction. That final write has a source-visible race with a concurrent restriction/account change. No reproduction or application fix was attempted. Enrollment itself does not require verified email, while new offers do. Do not claim every status change is equivalently guarded.
5. **Non-response exclusions:** timeout processor checks active/enrolled/member and current opportunity, but not the full live donor evaluator. Whether donors who became unavailable/unverified/age-ineligible should be exempt needs an explicit policy; do not silently assert that exemption.
6. **Automations:** callable safety/expiry/non-response/follow-up/retention and mail workers exist. Fresh scheduled-task inventory was unavailable; current flags, task health, cadence, catch-up and successful recurring execution were not certified. The old blanket statement that no scheduler exists is also unsupported.
7. **Notification scope:** approved queued coverage is narrower than baseline all-important-updates email wording. Availability edits lack a dedicated alert; post-window recovery has no proven alert; generic profile edits are not universal notification events. Maintain the actual event inventory instead of saying every event sends both channels.
8. **Demand/report acceptance:** deadline-aware aggregates and download exist. Map fetches on mount/filter/manual refresh, without a demonstrated periodic cadence. Today/last 7/30/365 days are UTC rolling date presets, not necessarily calendar week/month/year. Confirmed-donation summary counts confirmation dates, while other donation metrics can use report dates. These semantics must remain explicit.
9. **Privacy/security/retention:** AES-GCM document support, private DTOs, no-store streaming, consent and scoped evidence exist; configured encryption mode, actual encrypted files/keys, database/log/backup encryption, transport, restore-time deletion enforcement and device behavior remain unverified. Baseline permanent audit logs conflict with migration 026's reviewed one-year pruning with holds. AuditLogger still returns null on failure; some critical paths abort, others do not, so universal durable audit is not guaranteed.
10. **General administration:** chapter/configuration maintenance endpoints/UI are missing even though references/settings exist. Current user management covers chapter assignment and deactivation/reactivation, not arbitrary chapter creation/settings editing. Treat additional CRUD as baseline-planned, pending reconciliation with the single-admin scope.
11. **Removed/out-of-scope behavior:** password reset is removed (034); role promotion and separate officer authority are retired. Actual completed-donation reversal and per-member chat blocking are explicitly outside the revised approved plan. Completed correction means reasoned response/history, not reversal. No replacement recovery or transfer workflow should be invented.
12. **Acceptance claims:** mobile/accessibility/browser flows, parallel-action safety, <=3-second matching under load, 99.5% uptime, real delivery and software-quality scores remain unverified by this review. Current code and historical passes do not supply those measurements.
