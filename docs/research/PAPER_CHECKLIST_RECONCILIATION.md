## 4. Proposed revised scope

This study focuses on developing, testing and evaluating BloodMatch, a web-based coordination platform for DeMolay Bataan members. Members register and verify email, maintain permitted profiles and submit or resubmit identification. The designated Admin Officer reviews membership and supporting documents through controlled global staff access. Officer accounts are not created through public member registration.

The platform supports validated blood requests, compatible donor substitutions and eligibility checks, donor responses/withdrawal, requester offer acceptance and consented chat, donation occurrence/proof reporting, versioned requester acknowledgment and independent exception review. It preserves report history, holds and valid late records through request closure, expiry and linked renewal. The old donor chapter/municipality-search objective is explicitly superseded by approved chapter/post-urgency demand visualization. Existing donor search and ranking remain available; no new donor-municipality filter is required under the superseded objective. The stronger sequential-cascade requirement in the old feature baseline needs explicit reconciliation with the paper's compatible-substitution wording.

The paper's48h target is implemented in source for new opportunities, with the approved temporary exception retaining existing 42h deadlines and active standby. Eligibility exclusions and non-revival are offline verified; SQL/race and recurring-runtime verification remain pending. Configured post-donation windows, report-specific holds, report reminders at 24h and escalation at 48h retain their independent anchors and rules.

Monitoring includes privacy-safe chapter demand counts/units, Home chapter/post-urgency filters, staff demand controls, date/chapter activity analytics and authorized CSV/print/PDF. These demand filters do not implement donor search. Automatic real-time freshness, full-view chapter selection and calendar reporting periods must be specified and verified before claiming complete scope fulfillment. Notifications use the named in-app events and approved email classes; no all-event/all-channel guarantee is made.

The platform does not collect, store, transport or medically process blood, determine clinical eligibility, guarantee a donor/donation, permit unrestricted private-chat inspection or automatically reverse recorded donation credit. Registration, role access and audit-retention wording must reflect the later approved designated-admin and retention policies. Unimplemented configuration/chapter CRUD, independent-review fallback and other unresolved baseline features remain explicit scope decisions, not silently completed functions.
